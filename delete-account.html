SudoClash - Account & Data Deletion Request

At SudoClash, we respect your right to control your personal data and account information in accordance with Google Play, Amazon Appstore, and GDPR/CCPA requirements.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data is permanently erased from our Firebase database and servers within 7 business days:

  • User Profile: Your display name, email address, avatar URL, and associated unique User ID.
  • Multiplayer & Rating Records: Your PvP match history, Elo rating, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Game Statistics: Your recorded XP, levels, daily challenge completion history, and personal best times.
  • Authentication Token: Your Firebase Google Sign-In authentication profile.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion using either of the following methods:

Method 1: Email Deletion Request (Recommended)

Send an email from the Google account associated with your SudoClash profile to:

Support Email: sushantm95@gmail.com

Subject: SudoClash Account Deletion Request

Body: Please include your registered email address and display name so we can verify and delete your account.

Send Deletion Request Email

Method 2: In-App Deletion

  1. Open the SudoClash application on your device.
  2. Navigate to Settings ➔ Account.
  3. Tap Sign Out & Clear Local Data.
  4. Send a deletion request email above for server-side removal.

Data Retention Policy

Upon verification of your request, your account and all associated records are permanently purged from Firebase servers within 7 days. Data cannot be recovered once deletion is complete.
